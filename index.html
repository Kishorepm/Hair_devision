Welcome to Hair Division.

Established in 2008, our Takapuna hair salon combines expert hair care with a friendly and relaxed environment. We’re dedicated to high standards, personalised service and beautiful results.

Raine & Ivy

Meet the team.

Owner & senior stylist

Raine

With over 30 years of experience in the hairdressing industry, I’m proud to be the new owner of Hair Division Takapuna. I am lucky enough to take over the salon after my friend and colleague Drew has begun a new adventure in the South Island.

My journey began in Devonport, where I built a strong foundation in both classic and contemporary hairdressing, before continuing my career in Takapuna, where I’ve been part of the community for the past 17 years at Hair Division.

I genuinely enjoy every aspect of hairdressing, from precision cutting and creative colouring to the simple pleasure of helping someone feel confident and refreshed.

Apprentice stylist

Ivy

Ivy is in her second year as an apprentice at Hair Division. Ivy has a natural ability for hair and passion for colour and brings a fresh perspective on fashion techniques. Outside of the salon, Ivy loves hitting the gym, spending time with her cat and besties.
